# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot  
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. [DOC-3]  
- Q1 FY27 revenue was reported as ₹1,248 cr (press release) or ₹1,428 cr (news article), with YoY growth of 18 % or 35 % respectively. [DOC-3][DOC-1]  
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 cr as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 cr a year earlier. [DOC-3]  
- A new Bharuch plant, adding about 30 % to capacity, is slated to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [DOC-3][DOC-2]  

## Bull case  
- The order book is expanding and exports now account for 21 % of revenue, indicating diversified demand. [DOC-3]  
- The Bharuch expansion will boost capacity by roughly 30 % and is expected to contribute from the second half of FY27. [DOC-3][DOC-2]  
- Management maintains FY27 revenue‑growth guidance of 15–17 % and plans to pass copper‑price increases to customers. [DOC-2]  
- Receivables have lengthened to 96 days but no defaults have been reported. [DOC-2]  
- EBITDA margin remained at 11.2 % in Q1 FY27, only a 1.7 pp drop from FY26, showing margin resilience. [DOC-3]  

## Bear case  
- Copper prices rose 14 % in the quarter, contracting operating margin by 170 bps. [DOC-1][DOC-3]  
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, signalling slower collections. [DOC-2]  
- Promoter pledge figures conflict: 35 % pledged per a 2024 news article versus 4.1 % of promoter holding per the latest NSE filing, raising governance concerns. [DOC-4][DOC-8]  
- A GST demand order of ₹46.3 cr has been issued for FY20‑21 and FY21‑22; the company expects no material impact but the financial effect is unclear. [DOC-7]  
- A ₹8 lakh penalty was imposed on a local cable‑TV operator named Sarvottam Cable Network for illegal overhead wiring; its relation to the listed company is not specified. [DOC-6]  

## Open questions  
- Q1 FY27 revenue is reported as ₹1,248 cr in the press release and ₹1,428 cr in the news article; the reason for the discrepancy is not explained. [DOC-3][DOC-1]  
- Export share rose from 16 % to 21 %; the drivers of this increase are not detailed. [DOC-3]  
- The financial impact of the ₹46.3 cr GST demand order has not been quantified. [DOC-7]  
- It is unclear whether the Nagpur penalty applies to the listed Sarvottam Cables Ltd or a separate entity. [DOC-6]
